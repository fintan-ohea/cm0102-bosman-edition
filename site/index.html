What it adds

  • A Choose Season screen with a tile for each season. The tile picks the database, so you never install one by hand.
  • A database for every season comes in the download: the CM 01/02 Update Team's databases, and our own for 2004/05 and 2006/07. Each one unpacks the first time you pick its season.
  • The game starts in July of the season you pick. Every player has the right age for that July, and the competition history lists the real winners up to that season.
  • Each league has that season's number of clubs, promotion and relegation places, play-offs and splits. Domestic cups have that season's format.
  • The number of substitutes, foreign player rules, work permits and transfer windows follow the season. The bench holds at most nine players.
  • Competitions and their rounds carry the names they had that season, such as the Championship or the Europa League.
  • In a long game, each rule changes in the July it changed in real life, and a news item lists the changes. You can switch this off for a new game.
  • The Nations League, the 48-team World Cup and other international competitions come in from the year they started.
  • A Custom database tile for a database folder of your own. You tell it which season the data is from.
  • The base game stays: 2001/02 with the 3.9.68 data and the rule changes off. Saves from earlier versions load and play as before.

Europe by era

The Choose Season screen names the European format of the season you pick.

Early 2000s2001/02 to 2002/03

The Champions League has two group stages, the UEFA Cup has knockout rounds only, and the Intertoto Cup is played in summer.

Mid 2000s2003/04 to 2008/09

One Champions League group stage, then a round of 16. From 2004/05, the UEFA Cup has eight groups of five. The last Intertoto Cup is in 2008.

2010s2009/10 to 2019/20

The Europa League takes the place of the UEFA Cup and the Intertoto Cup. From 2015/16, the Europa League winner gets a Champions League place.

Early 2020s2020/21 to 2023/24

The Conference League starts in 2021/22. From 2022/23, its winner gets a place in the Europa League groups.

Mid 2020s2024/25 on

The Champions League, the Europa League and the Conference League each start with a league phase of 36 clubs.

The European cups, year by year
  • 2001/02Champions League: three qualifying rounds, a first group stage of 32 clubs, a second group stage of 16, then the quarter-finals. The eight group thirds go into the UEFA Cup.
  • 2001/02Intertoto Cup: five rounds over the summer, all two legs. Its three winners enter the UEFA Cup first round.
  • 2003/04Champions League: the second group stage goes. The group winners and runners-up play a round of 16.
  • 2004/05UEFA Cup: after the first round, 40 clubs play in eight groups of five, two matches at home and two away. The top three of each group and the eight Champions League group thirds play a round of 32.
  • 2009/10Europa League: 48 clubs in 12 groups of four. The Intertoto Cup ends. Champions League qualifying splits into a Champions Path for league champions and a League Path for the other clubs.
  • 2009/10The Champions League holder gets a group place even when its league finish is not good enough. The Europa League holder gets a Europa League group place.
  • 2015/16The Europa League winner goes into the Champions League, at least into its play-off round. From 2018/19 it goes straight into the groups.
  • 2021/22Europa League: 32 clubs in eight groups. The runners-up play the Champions League group thirds in a knockout play-off. The group thirds go down to the new Conference League.
  • 2024/25League phase: each cup has one table of 36 clubs. The top eight go to the round of 16, 9th to 24th play a knockout play-off, and 25th to 36th go out.
  • 2009/10 onThe places for each nation come from that season's UEFA access list.

Substitutes, bench, work permits and windows
  • Every league allows three substitutions in 2001/02. Germany, Italy and Spain allow five from 2020/21.
  • The Premier League bench has five players in 2001/02, seven from 2008/09 and nine from 2021/22. The game's bench holds nine at most, so Spain's bench of twelve plays as nine.
  • England has no quota on nationality. With changing rules on, the original's limit of three non-EU players in an English match squad goes.
  • In 2001/02 a player from outside the EU needs a work permit to play in England. From 1 January 2021 the points system covers EU players too, in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.
  • In 2001/02 England has a transfer deadline in spring and no windows. The windows from 2002/03 have each season's real dates, so the Premier League window shuts in early August in 2018/19 and 2019/20.

How a long game moves
  • Changing rules is a switch in Setup Game for each new game, and it is on by default. The save keeps it.
  • With it on, each rule changes in the July it changed in real life, also in a game that starts in 2001/02.
  • At the start of a season with new rules, a news item lists them, as in the picture above.
  • Your results decide which clubs go up and down. The number of places is the real one for that season.
  • After 2025/26, the changes already announced with a date come in, such as the Championship play-offs from 3rd to 8th. Then the rules stay as they are.
  • With changing rules off, the rules of your start season stay for the whole game. A game that starts in 2010/11 keeps a Euro of 16 nations.

How it knows

7,000+rule rows, one per competition and season
5,900+links to the pages they came from
39,000+facts checked against a second source

Each row holds a league's or a cup's rules for one season: the number of clubs, the places up and down, play-offs, the cup format, substitutes, foreign players, transfer windows and the name. The rows cover 27 nations, the European and other continental club cups, and international football. Each fact links to the page it came from, on more than 300 websites.

Every fact was then checked against a second source. Where a fact had only one, another pass looked for a second publisher. Where two sources disagreed, a third pass compared them and settled more than 1,000 rows. We decided the rest by hand.

How to install

Mac

  1. Download the .dmg file and open it.
  2. Drag the game onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open the game. If macOS says it cannot check it, go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Windows

  1. Download the Setup.exe file and run it.
  2. If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click through the installer. The game appears in the Start menu and on the desktop.

Linux and Steam Deck

  1. Download the .AppImage file. On a Steam Deck, do this in Desktop Mode.
  2. Right-click the file, open Properties and allow it to run as a program.
  3. Double-click the file to start the game. On the Steam Deck, the trackpad moves the mouse.

Questions

Is this official?

No. It is a fan project with no link to Sports Interactive, SEGA, Eidos or Square Enix. Eidos made the game free to download in 2008, and this download includes those game files.

How does it run on a modern computer?

Every instruction of the original Windows program is translated into C and compiled for your machine. No emulator runs underneath, so the match engine and the rest of the game are the ones from 2002.

Where are my saved games?

On a Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/cm0102. On Windows: %APPDATA%\cm0102. On Linux: ~/.local/share/cm0102. A new release does not change this folder. Older copies of each save are in the backups folder inside it.

How do I get the 800 × 600 layout back?

Open Bosman Edition Options from Setup Game or from Game Options, and set Display mode to Classic. Then start the game again. You can also change the line in settings.txt in the save folder to display = classic.

Which settings can I change in version 0.5.0?

Open Bosman Edition Options from Setup Game or from Game Options in the side bar, or open settings.txt in the save folder. Each option has one line in the file, and a switch takes on or off. The defaults are:

  • display = widescreen draws the game at 1280 × 800, and classic at 800 × 600.
  • space_to_continue = on lets the space bar press Continue Game.
  • shortcuts = on adds keys for Back, for closing a popup and for full screen.
  • sharp_picture = on scales the picture to the window in whole pixels, so that text stays crisp.
  • save_folder = is empty by default, which uses the normal folder. Set it to keep saves somewhere else, for example in iCloud Drive.
  • sound = on plays the sound. off mutes the game.
  • clickable_names = on lets you click a player or club name to open its profile.
  • attribute_colours = on colours attribute numbers by value.
  • faster_days = on makes days pass faster while you are on holiday.
  • match_columns = on shows each player's position in the Condition view of the tactics screen, in every layout. In widescreen it also shows position, condition and match rating together on the match screens.
  • save_backups = on keeps the last three copies of each save in the backups folder.
  • remember_window = on opens the window at the size and place it had when you quit.
  • condition_colours = on colours condition and morale: red, amber or green.
  • database = 3.9.68 is the database for the next new game. 3.9.60 is the original 2001/02 data and 3.9.65 its December 2001 update. folder:May 2026 is a folder of your own in Databases (see the FAQ). The Database button on the Setup Game screen changes this line.
  • squad_views = on adds Team views (best XI, squad depth, compare players) to the Assistant menu of every squad screen, and the polygon to the comparison screen.
  • attribute_groups = on shows the player profile's attributes in Technical, Mental and Physical groups.
  • foreign_colours = on shows in light violet the players who count against the foreign player limit of the club's next league or cup match.
  • full_player_search = off keeps the original player search. on lists every player, also the ones your manager does not know.

Prints go to the Prints folder as PDF files. To get the old text files, set Print To to File in the game's own options.

Is the database updated?

You can choose. The default is the data of the last official patch, 3.9.68, which has the 2002/03 squads. The original 2001/02 data and its December 2001 update are on the Database button of the Setup Game screen. Fan databases are not included, but you can play them: copy a database of the CM 01/02 Update Team (champman0102.co.uk) into a new folder in Databases in your save folder, for example Databases/May 2026. Copy every file of the database, euro.cfg included. The Database button then lists the folder by its name. The game starts in July 2001 whatever the database.

Something does not work

Open an issue on GitHub. Say which computer you use and what you clicked before the problem.
