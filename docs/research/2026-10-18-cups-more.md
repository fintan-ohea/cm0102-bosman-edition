## 1. Group stages

Formats (the group rows of `season_cups_more.c`):

- Scottish League Cup (`sco_lge_cup`) from 2016/17: 8 groups, single round robin in July, the 8 winners and the best runners-up join the clubs in Europe (byes) in a last 16, single matches with extra time and penalties.
- EFL Trophy (`eng_auto_cup`) from 2016/17: 16 groups of 4 and 3 (the 56 clubs that the class picks in a game of the October 2016 database, run e2016; which 8 clubs it adds to League One and League Two is not checked; the 16 academy sides are not in the game, Q336), the top 2 of each group to a last 32, penalties after 90 minutes, the final with extra time and penalties.
- Turkish Cup (`tur_cup`): the groups come after some of the class's rounds, because the class (TurCup 0x8fc670) holds 64 clubs. 2005/06 to 2010/11 and 2024/25 to 2025/26: 4 groups of 4 after rounds 1 to 3, top 2 to the quarter-finals. 2012/13 and 2013/14: 2 groups of 4 after rounds 1 to 4, top 2 to the semi-finals. 2014/15 to 2016/17: 8 groups of 4 after rounds 1 and 2, top 2 to a last 16.

How it works (the evidence is in the head comment of `season_cup_groups.c`): the object plays its own round table first (the base rounds), then `groups + 1` stages, as the Coppa Italia does (ItaCup: League_Stage groups from `create_group_stage` 0x63f640, then a Cup_Stage knockout from `create_knockout_stage` 0x63f870) and as slice 22 builds the UEFA Cup groups on the Cup base:

1. After the class's `create_round_table` (vtable +0x3c, stage -1) at the set-up of a season the object gets +0x2c = groups + 1. A set-up call passes the cup's own round count +0x3c (`Cup::set_up_rounds` 0x4c15f0, call at 0x4c160a; the constructors, for example ScoLgeCup at 0x7efad3, EngAutoCup at 0x56ab7e and its `set_up_rounds` at 0x56b71a, TurCup at 0x8fc489, RusCup at 0x7e9307). A caller that only reads the tables passes its own buffer, for example `Comp::build_match_date_list` 0x48d500 (the Schedule tab and scout duty, call at 0x48d5c4 with a count at esp + 0x16), and changes nothing. Before the fix of 2026-10-21 such a read deleted the groups of a running season and drew them again (`free_stages`). With base rounds the table keeps its first base rounds. The later slots are marked 0xff after `map_fixture_tree` 0x518790, or the base rounds never end (`process_fixture` 0x51a150 at 0x51b3c8 counts them done when no club has 0).
2. `Cup::update_for_date` 0x51f890 calls slot 10 (advance_stage) when +0xb1 is set and every stage up to +0x30 is complete (0x51f8da..0x51f8f5). Slot 10 with +0x30 = -1 draws the groups (League_Stage 0x68b100, one per group): the winners of the last base round and the clubs that had not played yet, less the byes (club +0x1db is the Champions League or the UEFA Cup, the test of `ScoLgeCup::collect_entrants` 0x7f0400), in pots by division level and last place.
3. Slot 10 with every group complete draws the knockout (Cup_Stage 0x522e00): the places that go through, the best clubs of the next place (points per match, goal difference, goals) and the byes, seeded clubs against the others, not of their own group when such a draw exists.
4. The honours come from the knockout stage (the wraps of `Cup::record_season_honours` 0x519a90 in `season_uefa_cup.c` and of 0x595160). A save keeps +0x2c, +0x30 and the stages (`Cup::save_cup_to_disk` 0x5210f0). The stage array is made before a load.

The EFL Trophy class (EngAutoCup) has two stages of its own: a regional half (`create_section_stage` 0x56bab0) and the final (`advance_stage` 0x56bc50). In a group season its team list takes the clubs of both halves, and its stage numbers that are fixed in the code are wrapped: stage 0 in `rank_teams` 0x56c010 (0x56c06b) and stage 1 in slot 12 0x595160.

Guards (review 2 of 2026-10-20):

- The big stage arrays are kept with their cup, and the list is cleared at each new game, load and end of a game (`season_cup_groups_reset` from `season_change_reset`): the classes' destructors free the arrays and malloc gives the addresses again.
- The knockout byes are the clubs of the team list that are in no group. The European mark (club +0x1db) is read once, at the group draw, so a club whose mark changes before the knockout draw is never both in a group and a bye.
- A group draw that cannot finish (more than 10 match days, or no memory) frees its groups, and the class's rounds play. With fewer clubs than the knockout needs, the knockout is the largest power of two that fits. Any other knockout failure ends the cup after its groups. Each path writes a log line. Without these, +0x30 stays and `Cup::update_for_date` calls slot 10 every day (0x51f8da..0x51f8f5), so the cup never ends.

Test seam: `RECOMP_CUP_GROUPS_TEST=1` gives the League Cup (`eng_cc_cup`) the row `test_eng_cc_cup`: 16 groups of 5 and 6, a last 32 in 5 rounds, in 2001/02 with England alone. The League Cup uses the same Cup base slots as the Scottish League Cup.

## 2. Four-club super cups

The Supercoppa Italiana (2023/24 to 2025/26) and the Supercopa de Espana (from 2019/20) have four clubs in the rows. ItaSuper (vtable 0x96c8b8) and SpaSuper (vtable 0x96fd38) are the same code: the constructor and `start_new_season` call the team picker (`init_team_list` 0x65fa60, `select_teams` 0x858aa0) and then `create_round_table` (0x65f920, 0x858960). The wraps of both give four clubs and two rounds: semi-finals (code 130, flag 0x200, so the ties are the list's pairs, `map_fixture_tree` 0x519279) and the final (code 150), one match each.

Decisions (recommended answers taken, for Max to confirm):

- The semi-finals are league winner v cup runner-up and cup winner v league runner-up.
- A cup place whose club already has a place goes to the league third, once. If that is not enough, two clubs play.
- Some match dates are an inference (marked in `season_cups_more.c`).
- The Spanish single match of 2018/19 is not built: the class's one match plays.

## 3. New cups

Six cups on new competition records of the module (`SeasonCompDef` in `season_new_cups.c`):

| File | Cup | Entry | Seasons |
|---|---|---|---|
| `new_tur_super` | Turkish Super Cup | 32 | from 2006/07, not 2011/12 (cancelled), four clubs from 2025/26 |
| `new_rus_super` | Russian Super Cup | 27 | from 2003 (March before the season), from 2012 in July |
| `new_nor_super` | Norwegian Super Cup | 24 | March 2009, 2010, 2017 and 2018 |
| `new_rus_lge_cup` | Russian Premier League Cup | 27 | 2003 |
| `new_conf_cup` | Conference League Cup | 14 | 2004/05, 2007/08, 2008/09 |
| `new_all_ireland` | All-Ireland cup | 20 | 2010/11 to 2013/14 |

Each object is an ItaSuper (constructor 0x65f670, size 0xb2 as `create_italian_comps` 0x835320 allocates it) with its own vtable copy (`season_comps_def_vtable`), so the season change, the rules report and the picker wraps know it by its file. The picker wraps give the clubs of its nation (the key id globals in `season_new_cups.c`). The Conference League Cup takes the data record "English Conference Cup" (record 95 in 3.9.68, no object). The others get a new record like the cup of their nation.

A season with no edition: the before hook of `start_new_season` (kind `new_cups`, listed in `season_change.c`) skips the cup (`season_change_skip`). When the format plays again, it sets +0x40 to S - 1, so `start_new_season` (0x65fc90, inc word [esi+0x40]) builds edition S with new dates. This is the third case of the shared rule in `season_comps.h`.

A calendar-year cup whose first match is already past at a new game (for example a March super cup at a July start) is not made in that season. The module asks again in the next season.

Before the game has its changing rules flag (the module's record step at `database_load` 0x528540 of a new game runs before `create_game` 0x828110 sets it), `new_held` takes the flag of the next new game, as `wanted` in `season_comps.c` does.

The module makes at most `MAX_NEW` (32) new records in a game: 13 defs have `like` on main at this merge. A def that finds no room writes a log line, and the ctest `season_comps_new_records` fails when the defs with `like` outgrow the limit.

With changing rules off, only the start season's hooks run (`season_change.c`, the pass of 0x5d5ae0), so the start hook makes the edition that the start season's row holds (Q232): for a cup in the row of the season before, the edition of the next calendar year. Examples: the Russian Premier League Cup of March 2003 at a 2002 start, the Russian Super Cup of March 2011 at a 2010 start. After that edition the cup plays every year with the start season's format, as every cup of the start season does with changing rules off.

Module blocks of this part in slice 26's files: `SeasonCompDef.held`, a comp written "Nation:comp" (a comp name alone matched several nations), `season_comps_def_vtable`, `_file_of`, `_base_vtable` and `_def_held`, the fall-back to the def's file in `season_change_file` and in the report's class lookup, and `NEW_FILES` and `row_files` in `rules_report_check.py`.

## 4. Not built (decisions for Max)

Recommended answers taken:

- The Scottish League Cup bonus point for a penalty win after a draw needs a match engine change. Not built: a draw stays a draw.
- The EFL Trophy has no academy sides (Q336). It plays 16 groups with the class's 56 clubs (8 groups of 4, 8 of 3), nearer the real 16 groups of 4 than 16 groups of 3 of the League clubs only (2026-10-20, run e2016).
- The Turkish groups fit the class's 64 clubs (section 1): the real groups of 5, 6 or 8 clubs need more clubs than the class holds at that point.
- The Russian Cup group seasons from 2020/21 are not built. Recommendation: keep the knockout.
- The National League Cup from 2024/25 is not played (Q336).
- The All-Ireland group seasons (2004/05 to 2007/08 and 2009/10) are not played.
- The Coppa Italia and the KNVB Cup keep their group stages in every season (ItaCup and HolCup always start with `create_group_stage`, 0x63f640 and 0x5f4220). The real group stages ended in 2005/06 and 2003/04. Recommendation: a later pass gives them a knockout from the first round.
- Groups after the eighth repeat the names from "Group A".
- The new cups all use the class ItaSuper.
