# Scouting: knowledge, scouts, duties and results

Research for `docs/specs/2026-10-24-auto-scouting-spec.md`. Original 3.9.68, addresses of the original program, read from the disassembly in the generated code (`build/recomp/gen`) and from runs of the fork's build (start.sav of `tests/fixtures`, Arsenal, 14.7.01).

## What a manager knows

- FOG_OF_WAR (`[0xae2a60]`): FOG_OF_WAR::get_manager_map (0x5afde0) gives a manager's map; FOG_OF_WAR_MAP::get_knowledge_level (0x5b02f0) gives 0 to 3 for a person; raise_knowledge (0x5b09d0) and daily_update (0x5b0260) change it.
- staff_is_player_attribute_visible (0x543280): with attribute masking on (`[0x9cc270]`), level 3 shows every attribute; level 2 and 1 hide a set that depends on the player's id (0x5432db..0x5433a3); level 0 is handled further on.

## A scout's judging

The non-player attributes (names database_get_non_player_attribute_name 0x543b10, jump table 0x543ef8; values staff_compute_non_player_attribute 0x543fd0, jump table 0x5442b4) of the coaching data `c` = staff `+0x69`:

| Index | Name | Byte |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adaptability | staff +0x56 or c+0x11 (two cases at 0x54404f) |
| 2 | Coaching Goalkeepers | c+0x11 |
| 3 | Coaching | c+0x10 |
| 4 | Determination | staff +0x58 or c+0x17 (0x544144) |
| 5 | Judging Player Ability | c+0x17 |
| 6 | Judging Player Potential | c+0x18 |
| 7 | Discipline | c+0x14 or c+0x19 (0x544186) |
| 8 | Man Management | c+0x19 |
| 9 | Motivating | c+0x1b |
| 10 | Physiotherapy | c+0x1e |
| 11 | Tactics | c+0x21 |
| 12 | Working With Youngsters | c+0x22 |

The names are the short names of the table (Ada, CGk, Coa, Det, Jud, JPt, Dis, Man, Mot, Phy, Tct, Yng) and the long ones at 0x9b7c28 and 0x9b7c08 for 5 and 6. Indices 1, 4, 7 read two bytes; which one counts is not read yet. Coaching, Coaching Goalkeepers and Tactics are the bytes that update_coach_assignments (0x8b2880) uses (training research note).

## Scout duties

- SCOUT_MANAGER is `[0xb74448]` (the `this` of the calls of get_search 0x7f6cd0 and daily_update 0x7f8750). get_search (slot 0 to 9, manager): `[this] + ((manager id - [0xae2364] + 16) * 10 + slot) * 0x2b0` (0x7f6d3f..0x7f6d74). The Player & Staff Search screen shows them as Short List, Player Search, Staff Search and Scout #1 to #7.
- PLAYER_SEARCH, 0x2b0 bytes (constructor 0x7ae390): `+0x00` the manager id, `+0x0a` the list type (4, set by the constructor at 0x7ae432; start_scout_assignment needs 4, 0x7aea3d), `+0x0b` the search's name, `+0x180` the scout's staff id (-1 none) with his employment at `+0x174`..`+0x184` (get_scout 0x7ae950 checks it with 0x53e990).
- The duty: `+0x18c` nation (-1), `+0x190` club (-1, set_assigned_club 0x7b3d50), `+0x194` region (byte, -1), `+0x195` competition (-1), `+0x199` youth (0), `+0x19d` next opposition (0), `+0x1a1` (-1); `+0x1ad` the end date and `+0x1b5` a count of the duty's stages. clear_scout_assignment (0x7aee80) puts these back (0x7aef07..0x7aef47); run_scout_day (0x7b3e20) calls it when the duty ends (0x7b59e1).
- PLAYER_SEARCH::start_scout_assignment (0x7ae9c0), thiscall (nation, region, competition, youth, opposition), `ret 0x14`: the first argument that applies sets the duty: a nation index below `[0xae2350]` ("{nation} search", 0xa70240), a region 1 to 14 ("{region} search", 0xa70220), a competition index below `[0xae2378]`, youth not 0 ("Youth search", 0xa701f0), opposition not 0 ("Next Opposition", 0xa701e0). The duty lasts 3 days and a random number more (0x90cfc0 at 0x7aeaf2), and ends with build_scout_targets (0x7b0050).
- The screen's Assign Scout menu offers a current competition, Club, Next Opposition, Youth Players, Nation and Region.

## What the scouts find

- The results: `+0x1ee` a pointer to entries of 0x2d bytes, `+0x1f2` the room, `+0x1f6` the count (PLAYER_SEARCH::add_result 0x7af870: grows by 0xfa entries, 0x7af939; sorted by 0x7bce50). An entry (search_result_copy 0x7af580): `+0x0c` the person's staff id, `+0x10` and `+0x18` dates, `+0x29` ability stars and `+0x2a` potential stars (the comparers 0x819620 and 0x8196e0), `+0x2c` 0xff when 0x795750 refuses the person (0x7afa07).
- PLAYER_SEARCH::report_on_player (0x7b62a0) raises the manager's knowledge of the player (0x5b09d0 at 0x7b64cc) and sets the stars (0x7b6a47, 0x7b6a4e; 0x7b6bc4, 0x7b6bcb) with random numbers (0x90cfc0) and the scout's coaching data `+0x18` (Judging Player Potential, 0x7b661d..0x7b66c4). So the stars hold the scout's judgement. SCOUT_MANAGER::daily_update calls run_scout_day (0x7b3e20) and report_on_player.

## Search filters

- A search's filters are at `+0x1fa` (the constructor 0x7ae390 calls 0x806450 on them). The first 8 bytes are a 64-bit mask of options, 0x14140081 and 0x44aaa884 by default; set_option_flag (0x806630, thiscall (low, high), `ret 8`) sets an option and clears the rest of its group. The positions are bits of the high word: Goalkeeper 0x8, Defender 0x10, Midfielder 0x20, Attacker 0x40 (the filters screen, 0x8133a0, at 0x816fb7..0x8171c7).
- player_matches_filter (0x807450) has one caller, PLAYER_SEARCH::get_result_list (0x7af40d): the filters choose what a list of results shows, not what a scout looks at (run_scout_day 0x7b3e20 and build_scout_targets 0x7b0050 do not read them).

## Value and wage

- A person's value is the int32 at staff `+0x52`, the value that search_screens_compare_staff_by_value (0x819f70) sorts by (0x819fa6); contract_manager_calc_player_value (0x4d0c60) sets it for a regen (0x7a6f10).
- The weekly wage is the contract's `+0x0c` (CONTRACT_MANAGER::get_wage 0x4d0c20; `docs/research/2026-10-24-player-editor-attributes.md`).
