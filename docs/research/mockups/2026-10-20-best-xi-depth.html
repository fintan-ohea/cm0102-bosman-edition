Best XI: one screen with the best three at each position

Three ways to show Best XI and Squad depth together, in widescreen (1280 x 800). The data is an example: Arsenal in July 2001, 4-4-2. Each card holds the player that Best XI picks first, then the next two for that position. The stars show the score for the position (1 to 20) as half stars, 0.5 to 5, as Max asked on 2026-10-20. Players who cannot play the next match show in grey with the reason. The two DC slots, and the two FC slots, share one card, as in Squad depth today. The tabs become Best XI and Compare players.

B. Upright pitch, as the tactics screen

C. Shirts on the pitch, the next two under each
