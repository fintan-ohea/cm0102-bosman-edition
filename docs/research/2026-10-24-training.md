# Training: schedules, players and workload

Research for `docs/specs/2026-10-24-training-assistant-spec.md`. Original 3.9.68, addresses of the original program, read from the disassembly in the generated code (`build/recomp/gen`) and from runs of the fork's build. It checks the forum topic "How Training Really Works" (alex91, champman0102.co.uk t=11012, 2026-10-11, written with ChatGPT) claim by claim; a claim not listed here is not checked yet.

## The training manager

- `[0xde1f44]` is the training manager (the `this` of the calls at 0x5cf367, 0x5d3786, 0x5d6b48).
- TRAINING_MANAGER::get_manager_schedule (0x8b3930), (manager, index 0 to 9): `this + (h * 10 + index + 7) * 0x9b`, where h = `[0xae2364] - manager id - 1`, the manager's place among the 16 human staff at the end of the staff table (0x8b3934..0x8b3973). An index outside 0..9 becomes 1 (0x8b3954..0x8b395d).
- A run (start.sav of `tests/fixtures`, Arsenal, 14.7.01) shows a human manager's ten slots: 0 "Overview" (type -1), 1 No Training (type 0, all 0), 2 General (1: 25 in four categories), 3 Fitness (2), 4 Shooting (3), 5 Skill (4), 6 Tactics (5), 7 Gk (6: 10, 10, 0, 10, 50), 8 and 9 "Unused" (type 7, all 0). `+0x00` of each holds its index. The Training screen shows slots 1 to 9 as its tabs, the two unused ones as "Unused".
- A player's schedule: 6 bytes per player id at `[this + 0x651d] + id * 6` (TRAINING_MANAGER::set_player_schedule 0x8b2250: 0x8b2274..0x8b2288), byte 0 the slot. set_player_schedule, thiscall (staff, slot), `ret 8` (0x8b2335), first calls TRAINING_MANAGER::update_player_training (0x8b1750) when the slot changes, then writes it (0x8b229c). Bytes 3 to 5 hold signed 4-bit progress values (data map, 0x8b3640).
- TRAINING_MANAGER::get_player_schedule (0x8b39f0) uses the stored slot only when the player's club (contract 0x4cf5a0, `+4`) has a human manager (club `+0xcf`, an id among the last 16, 0x8b3a7b..0x8b3a95); an injured player (0x62c400 status 2, 0x8b3aa7) gets No Training. So the Training screen lists injured players under No Training while their slot stays as it was.
- A player who joins the squad has slot 0 in his entry: in the run two new players (staff 10420 and 10421) appeared in the first team a day after the assistant's Apply, on slot 0.

## A schedule

TRAINING_SCHEDULE, 0x9b bytes (constructor 0x8b4e10):

- `+0x04` to `+0x08`: the levels of Fitness, Tactics, Shooting, Skills and Goalkeeping (get_category_level 0x8b4f50, set_category_level 0x8b4f30 `ret 8`, which also sets `+0x93` to 0). The order is that of training_schedule_get_category_name (0x8b5520): Fitness (0xa8cf74), Tactics (0xa8cf48), Shooting (0xa8cf1c), then Skills and Goalkeeping (0xa8cec0 "...keeping").
- `+0x09`: the name, at most 11 characters and the end (set_default_name 0x8b50f0 copies 0xb bytes, 0x8b51f4..0x8b520b).
- `+0x15`: the type (set_type 0x8b4e50, set_default_name): 0 No Training, 1 General, 2 Fitness, 3 Shooting, 4 Skill, 5 Tactics, 6 Gk, 7 unused, 8 the manager's own (no default name).
- `+0x16`: position training flags (set_position_training 0x8b5090, set_side_training 0x8b50c0).

## Levels

The levels are 0, 10, 25 and 50: set_type writes them (0x8b4e71 0x19, 0x8b4e86 0xa, 0x8b4e88 0x32), and training_schedule_get_intensity_name (0x8b5690) names them by these values (0x8b56eb `cmp al, 0xa`, 0x8b5715 `0x19`, 0x8b573f `0x32`). The forum topic's None 0, Light 10, Medium 25, Intensive 50 is right.

## Workload

TRAINING_SCHEDULE::get_training_load (0x8b4f60): the whole numbers of `Fitness * -1.3` ([0x96f878]), `Goalkeeping * 0.5` ([0x968968]) and `Skills`, `Shooting`, `Tactics * -0.5` ([0x969540]), added with signs to give 1.3 x Fitness + 0.5 x each other category, at most 100 (0x8b4fe7..0x8b4ff0). The rounding is 0x945640, which sets the FPU to chop (`or ah, 0xc` at 0x94564f) before `fistp`: each term is cut, not rounded. So Medium Fitness is 32 and a Medium category 12.

- The forum topic's formula is right, but its examples are not: its outfield schedule (Medium, Intensive, Medium, Intensive, None) gives 94, not 95, and its goalkeeper schedule (Medium, Medium, None, Medium, Intensive) 81, not 82.5. A run of the assistant logs 94 and 81 from the game's own get_training_load.

## When training is applied

Training is not applied each day. TRAINING_MANAGER::update_player_training (0x8b1750) brings one player up to date: it takes the days since the day stored in his entry (`[entry + 1]`, 0x8b1bd5) and trains him for them with his schedule as it is now. Its callers are Injury::add_injury (0x62d312), MATCH_PLAYER::setup (0x6eb045), set_player_schedule (0x8b2297), and screens that show the player: the squad list (0x45ae98), squad numbers (0x478d87), the profile (0x87a720, 0x879a6a), the Training screen (0x8b79f1) and 0x47fd42.

The game's own Training screen does not bring players up to date before it changes a schedule or the coaches: TRAINING_EDIT_SESSION::apply_changes (0x8b0b40) copies the edited schedules over the manager's (`rep movsd` at 0x8b0b85), calls set_player_schedule only for players whose slot changes (0x8b0bb5), and calls set_coach_assignments (0x8b0bda), which copies the table (0x8b244a) and calls update_coach_assignments (0x8b2454). So a change of levels counts, for each player, from his last update.

## Training quality

In update_player_training, for each category: quality = `2 * level + 60` (`lea esi, [ecx + ecx + 0x3c]`, 0x8b1da1) plus `(coaching factor - 100) / 5` (0x8b1dde..0x8b1df4), held between 0 and 200 (0x8b1df6..0x8b1e0b). The positive branch needs quality above 100 (`cmp bp, 0x64`, 0x8b1ef2), the negative branch looks at quality below 110 (`cmp bp, 0x6e`, 0x8b1f36). The limits of the progress value are staff `+0x58` / 3 (Determination, attribute 10) and staff `+0x5b` / 3 - 6 (0x8b1e0d..0x8b1e41; the forum topic names `+0x5b` Professionalism, which no screen shows). The forum topic's formula, threshold and limits are right.

## Which attributes each category trains

TRAINING_MANAGER::apply_training_progress (0x8b2be0) writes the player data in five blocks, which match the categories by their attributes (names from `docs/research/2026-10-24-player-editor-attributes.md`):

| Category | Block | Attributes a screen shows | Bytes no screen shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fitness | 0x8b2e5c..0x8b2f5b | Acceleration +0x1b, Agility +0x1d, Jumping +0x2e, Pace +0x36, Reflexes +0x3a, Stamina +0x3c, Strength +0x3d | +0x34 |
| Tactics | 0x8b3055..0x8b30f2 | Decisions +0x24, Marking +0x32, Off The Ball +0x33, Positioning +0x39, Teamwork +0x3f | |
| Shooting | 0x8b2fa8..0x8b3008 | Finishing +0x27, Long Shots +0x31 | +0x38 |
| Skills | 0x8b2cf5..0x8b2e11 | Crossing +0x23, Dribbling +0x26, Set Pieces +0x29, Heading +0x2b, Passing +0x37, Tackling +0x3e, Technique +0x40 | +0x22, +0x41 |
| Goalkeeping | 0x8b313a..0x8b317e | Handling +0x2a | +0x35 |

This is the forum topic's list. Its names for the bytes no screen shows (Natural Fitness, Penalties, Corners, Throw-ins, One-on-ones) are not checked; the block of each category is an inference from its attributes, not from a read of the arguments.

## Coaches

- The coach table of a human manager: `[0xde1f44] + 0x652d + h * 54`, six records of 9 bytes (TRAINING_MANAGER::get_coach_assignments 0x8b4cd0: 0x8b4d20..0x8b4d26): s32 staff id (-1 for none), then one byte per category, 0 when the coach does not take it, else his contribution.
- TRAINING_MANAGER::update_coach_assignments (0x8b2880), thiscall (club, manager or 0), `ret 8`: drops records of staff without coaching data (staff `+0x69`), puts the assistant manager (0x53cb80) and the coaches (0x53cc30) into free records with all five categories (`0x01010101` and a byte, 0x8b29fd), then works out the contribution of each coach in each of his categories (jump table 0x8b2bc0) from his coaching data `c` = staff `+0x69`, with `R(x)` = non_player_attribute_rating 0x544410 (c, x):
  - Fitness (0x8b2b31): `(R(c+0x10) - c+0x12 + 21) / 2`
  - Tactics (0x8b2add): `(R(c+0x21) + R(c+0x10) + c+0x12) / 3`
  - Shooting (0x8b2aac): `(R(c+0x10) + c+0x12 + c+0x0e) / 3`
  - Skills (0x8b2b14): `(R(c+0x10) + c+0x12) / 2`
  - Goalkeeping (0x8b2b51): `R(c+0x11)`
  then divides by 1.0, 1.2, 1.4, 1.6 or 2.0 for a coach in 1 to 5 categories (floats at 0x8b2892..0x8b28b2, `fdiv` at 0x8b2b6e) and holds it between 1 and 20 (0x8b2b77..0x8b2b83). The forum topic's formulas and divisors are right; its names (`+0x10` Coaching, `+0x12` Coaching Technique, `+0x0e` Attacking, `+0x21` Tactics, `+0x11` Coaching Goalkeepers) fit the formulas but are its own.
- TRAINING_MANAGER::get_coaching_factor (0x8b41d0), thiscall (club, category), `ret 8`: 100 for a club without a human manager (0x8b42b8). Else the sum of the contributions in the category, divided by 1.0, 1.45, 1.7, 1.9, 2.05 or 2.2 for 1 to 6 coaches (floats at 0x8b41e0..0x8b4208, `fdiv` at 0x8b4283), times 40/7 (`[0x970400]` = 5.714285714285714), at least 50 (0x8b42a4). The forum topic's divisors are right.
