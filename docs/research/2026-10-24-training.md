# Training: schedules, players and workload

Research for `docs/specs/2026-10-24-training-assistant-spec.md`. Original 3.9.68, addresses of the original program, read from the disassembly in the generated code (`build/recomp/gen`) and from runs of the fork's build. It checks the forum topic "How Training Really Works" (alex91, champman0102.co.uk t=11012, 2026-10-11, written with ChatGPT) claim by claim; a claim not listed here is not checked yet.

## The training manager

- `[0xde1f44]` is the training manager (the `this` of the calls at 0x5cf367, 0x5d3786, 0x5d6b48).
- TRAINING_MANAGER::get_manager_schedule (0x8b3930), (manager, index 0 to 9): `this + (h * 10 + index + 7) * 0x9b`, where h = `[0xae2364] - manager id - 1`, the manager's place among the 16 human staff at the end of the staff table (0x8b3934..0x8b3973). An index outside 0..9 becomes 1 (0x8b3954..0x8b395d).
- A run (start.sav of `tests/fixtures`, Arsenal, 14.7.01) shows a human manager's ten slots: 0 "Overview" (type -1), 1 No Training (type 0, all 0), 2 General (1: 25 in four categories), 3 Fitness (2), 4 Shooting (3), 5 Skill (4), 6 Tactics (5), 7 Gk (6: 10, 10, 0, 10, 50), 8 and 9 "Unused" (type 7, all 0). `+0x00` of each holds its index. The Training screen shows slots 1 to 9 as its tabs, the two unused ones as "Unused".
- A player's schedule: 6 bytes per player id at `[this + 0x651d] + id * 6` (TRAINING_MANAGER::set_player_schedule 0x8b2250: 0x8b2274..0x8b2288), byte 0 the slot. set_player_schedule, thiscall (staff, slot), `ret 8` (0x8b2335), first calls TRAINING_MANAGER::update_player_training (0x8b1750) when the slot changes, then writes it (0x8b229c). Bytes 3 to 5 hold signed 4-bit progress values (data map, 0x8b3640).
- TRAINING_MANAGER::get_player_schedule (0x8b39f0) uses the stored slot only when the player's club (contract 0x4cf5a0, `+4`) has a human manager (club `+0xcf`, an id among the last 16, 0x8b3a7b..0x8b3a95); an injured player (0x62c400 status 2, 0x8b3aa7) gets No Training. So the Training screen lists injured players under No Training while their slot stays as it was.
- A player who joins the squad has slot 0 in his entry: in the run two new players (staff 10420 and 10421) appeared in the first team a day after the assistant's Apply, on slot 0.

## A schedule

TRAINING_SCHEDULE, 0x9b bytes (constructor 0x8b4e10):

- `+0x04` to `+0x08`: the levels of Fitness, Tactics, Shooting, Skills and Goalkeeping (get_category_level 0x8b4f50, set_category_level 0x8b4f30 `ret 8`, which also sets `+0x93` to 0). The order is that of training_schedule_get_category_name (0x8b5520): Fitness (0xa8cf74), Tactics (0xa8cf48), Shooting (0xa8cf1c), then Skills and Goalkeeping (0xa8cec0 "...keeping").
- `+0x09`: the name, at most 11 characters and the end (set_default_name 0x8b50f0 copies 0xb bytes, 0x8b51f4..0x8b520b).
- `+0x15`: the type (set_type 0x8b4e50, set_default_name): 0 No Training, 1 General, 2 Fitness, 3 Shooting, 4 Skill, 5 Tactics, 6 Gk, 7 unused, 8 the manager's own (no default name).
- `+0x16`: position training flags (set_position_training 0x8b5090, set_side_training 0x8b50c0).

## Levels

The levels are 0, 10, 25 and 50: set_type writes them (0x8b4e71 0x19, 0x8b4e86 0xa, 0x8b4e88 0x32), and training_schedule_get_intensity_name (0x8b5690) names them by these values (0x8b56eb `cmp al, 0xa`, 0x8b5715 `0x19`, 0x8b573f `0x32`). The forum topic's None 0, Light 10, Medium 25, Intensive 50 is right.

## Workload

TRAINING_SCHEDULE::get_training_load (0x8b4f60): the whole numbers of `Fitness * -1.3` ([0x96f878]), `Goalkeeping * 0.5` ([0x968968]) and `Skills`, `Shooting`, `Tactics * -0.5` ([0x969540]), added with signs to give 1.3 x Fitness + 0.5 x each other category, at most 100 (0x8b4fe7..0x8b4ff0). The rounding is 0x945640, which sets the FPU to chop (`or ah, 0xc` at 0x94564f) before `fistp`: each term is cut, not rounded. So Medium Fitness is 32 and a Medium category 12.

- The forum topic's formula is right, but its examples are not: its outfield schedule (Medium, Intensive, Medium, Intensive, None) gives 94, not 95, and its goalkeeper schedule (Medium, Medium, None, Medium, Intensive) 81, not 82.5. A run of the assistant logs 94 and 81 from the game's own get_training_load.
