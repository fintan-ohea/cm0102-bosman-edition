## 1. Hosts and the host-place rule

- The final tournament is in the UK and Ireland, 9 June to 9 July 2028, 24 teams. Sources: U3, U1 (read 2026-10-23).
- The hosts appointed on 10 October 2023 were England, Northern Ireland, Republic of Ireland, Scotland and Wales. Sources: U4, U3 (read 2026-10-23).
- Northern Ireland does not host matches, because the Casement Park venue was dropped. Source: W2 (read 2026-10-23). UEFA's 2025 and 2026 pages list four host nations (England, Republic of Ireland, Scotland, Wales). See disagreements below.
- Four hosts (England, Republic of Ireland, Scotland, Wales) also play in qualifying, in separate groups. Sources: U1, N1, W1 (read 2026-10-23).
- Number of kept places: two. A host that does not qualify through its group takes one of them if it is among the two best-ranked such hosts.
- UEFA rule, quote: "Two spots in the final tournament will be reserved for the two best-ranked host nations" (rest of the sentence: those that have not qualified as group winners or best runners-up). Source: U1 (read 2026-10-23).
- Second source, quote: "Two places in the final tournament will be reserved for two host countries with the best ranking". Source: N1 (read 2026-10-23).
- Wikipedia says the spots go to the hosts ranked highest in the overall European Qualifiers ranking. Source: W1 (read 2026-10-23). The ranking basis is in Wikipedia only. U1 says "best-ranked" without naming the ranking.

## 2. Qualifying draw

- Sunday 6 December 2026, International Convention Centre, Belfast, Northern Ireland. Sources: U2, U3, N1, W1 (read 2026-10-23).
- Seeding by the interim 2026-27 Nations League ranking. Hosts and Northern Ireland are kept apart. Source: W1 only (read 2026-10-23).

## 3. Group stage

- Teams entered: 54 scheduled (Wikipedia infobox). Source: W1 only (read 2026-10-23). UEFA pages read do not give a team count.
- Groups: 12 groups of four or five teams, home and away. Sources: U1, U2, U3, N1, W1 (read 2026-10-23).
- Sizes: six groups of four (A to F) and six of five (G to L). Source: W1 only (read 2026-10-23). 6x4 + 6x5 = 54, which fits.
- Direct qualifiers: the 12 group winners and the 8 best-ranked runners-up, 20 teams. Sources: U1, N1, W1 (read 2026-10-23).
- Window: March 2027 to November 2027. Sources: U2, U3, N1 (read 2026-10-23). No late-2026 matches found.
- Matchdays per W1 only (read 2026-10-23): MD1 26-27 Mar 2027, MD2 29-30 Mar, MD3 11-12 Jun, MD4 14-15 Jun, MD5 23-25 Sep, MD6 26-28 Sep, MD7 29 Sep-2 Oct, MD8 3-5 Oct, MD9 11-13 Nov, MD10 14-16 Nov. UEFA pages read give only the months. Another search result quoted W1 as "25 March 2027 to 28 March 2028" for the whole process. This differs by one day from the 26 March start above. Check W1 directly.
- Per-matchday dates on UEFA.com: not found (as of 2026-10-23).

## 4. Play-offs

- Entrants: the remaining runners-up and the best-ranked 2026-27 UEFA Nations League group winners that did not qualify. Sources: U1, N1 (read 2026-10-23). W1 describes them as the worst runners-up plus Nations League qualifiers.
- Quote: "The remaining spots will be assigned via play-offs between the remaining runners-up" (continues with the Nations League group winners). Source: U1 (read 2026-10-23).
- Places: 2 to 4, depending on how many host places are used. Sources: U1, N1, W1 (read 2026-10-23).
  - Both host places used: 8 teams, 2 paths of 4, 2 places. Single-leg semi-finals and finals.
  - One host place used: 12 teams, 3 paths of 4, 3 places. Single-leg semi-finals and finals.
  - No host place used: 8 teams, 4 home-and-away ties, 4 places.
- Seeding detail (W1 only): in the no-host case the four worst runners-up are seeded and play the second leg at home.
- Dates: March 2028. Sources: U2, U3, N1 (read 2026-10-23).
- Exact dates per W1 only (read 2026-10-23): semi-finals 23-25 March 2028, finals 26-28 March 2028. A search summary quoted other sites with "23-28 March" and "24-28 March". Sources disagree by a day.
- Exact play-off dates on UEFA.com: not found (as of 2026-10-23).

## 5. Russia

- Background: in May 2022 UEFA extended the suspension and deemed Russia's Euro 2028/32 bid ineligible. Quote: "That means that Russia's bid for Euro 2028/32 has been deemed ineligible." Source: N2 (read 2026-10-23).
- No UEFA.com 2026 page read mentions Russia. Russia's status is one source (W1) for 2026.

## What the game plays (season_nl_euro.c, Nations League table row euro2028)

The two sources of the host rule: U1 (https://www.uefa.com/euro2028/news/0299-1dcf3fef69a9-41405d004b47-1000--qualification-system-for-uefa-euro-2028-approved/, read 2026-10-23) and N1 (https://uaf.ua/en/article/54992, read 2026-10-23). The play-off shapes have the same two sources and W1.

- Groups: the 10 groups of slice 27 (season_intl_euroq.c), the top two of each group qualify, 20 places. The real 12 groups give 20 places too (12 winners and 8 best runners-up). The class holds at most 10 groups.
- Group matchdays: the ten days of section 3 (the first day of each window), from March to November 2027 (dates file, events euro2028_qual_md1 to md10).
- Hosts: England, Republic of Ireland, Scotland and Wales play the qualifying. Northern Ireland is not one of them (section 1 and the disagreements).
- Host places: when the groups are over, the hosts that did not qualify are ranked by group place, then points (the ranking of group_rows). The first two take the two kept places through EuroChamp::add_qualified_team 0x57c120, the call that a group qualifier gets (0x57d609). Inference: this ranking stands for the overall qualifying ranking that only W1 names.
- Play-off, by the host places in use:
  - 2: 2 paths of 4, single-leg semi-finals (1 v 4, 2 v 3) and a final, 2 places.
  - 1: 3 paths of 4, the same rounds, 3 places.
  - 0: 4 two-leg ties of 8 nations, 4 places. The first leg on 23 March 2028 and the second 3 days later (round +0x22), no away goals. The better nation of each tie plays the second leg at home (W1).
- Play-off dates: semi-finals (or first legs) 23 March 2028, finals (or second legs) 26 March 2028, the first days of the W1 windows. The Euro's seeding and draw move to 28 and 30 March 2028.
- Play-off nations: in the game every runner-up qualifies from the groups, so no runner-up is left for the play-off. The paths take the nations that did not qualify by the 2026-27 Nations League ranking (season_nl_paths, as Euro 2020 and 2024), or by group place and points when the game holds no 2026-27 tables. Decision: the real entrants are the remaining runners-up and the best non-qualified Nations League group winners, and the class has no remaining runners-up.
- 20 + 2 + 2, 20 + 1 + 3 and 20 + 0 + 4 all give the 24 places, so the finals fill does not act in a full qualifying.
- Test switch RECOMP_TEST_EURO_HOST_PLACES=0 or 1 caps the host places, so a run plays the other two play-off shapes.
