# UEFA club competitions 2009/10 to 2020/21 (0.6.0 slice 23)

Date 2026-10-18. Original: `clean-3968/cm0102.exe` 3.9.68, virtual addresses. Code: `src/recomp_rt/win/season_uefa_seeding.c` (places), `season_uefa_qual.c` (qualifying rounds, paths, losers to the Europa League, the Intertoto Cup that is not held), `season_uefa_cup.c` (group stage of any size), `season_uefa_super_cup.c` (venue), `season_uefa_lookup.c` and `season_uefa_table.c` (the table, made by `tools/seasons/make_uefa_table.py`). Research of the facts: `docs/research/rules-0.6.0/uefa.tsv`, `uefa-access.tsv` and `docs/research/2026-10-18-uefa-access-and-round-names.md`. Slice 22's doc: `2026-10-17-uefa-formats.md`.

The format of a season comes from the table. The base game, a game with `RECOMP_SEASON_CODE=off` and every season before 2009/10 keep what they had.

## The table

`make_uefa_table.py` reads, for 2001/02 to 2020/21:

- the rows of `uefa.tsv` as slice 22 did, and: qualifying rounds of two paths (`Q3 CP`, `Q3 LP`, `Q2 MP`), the clubs that go through a group (`top two|three plus`), the Super Cup stadium, city and date (`one match at <stadium>, <city>, <day> <month> <year>;`);
- `uefa-access.tsv` from 2009/10: for each rank bracket the entry round of each place in domestic order, the title holder rows, the fair play rows, and the league cup winners of England and France (from the notes);
- `DROPS` of the tool: where the losers of the Champions League rounds go. 2009/10 to 2017/18: Q3 losers to the play-offs, play-off losers to the groups (the Europa League rows: "33 from the Champions League" = 15 + 10 + 8). 2018/19 on: preliminary and Q1 losers to Q2, Q2 losers to Q3, Champions Path Q3 losers to the play-offs, League Path Q3 losers and play-off losers to the groups ("55 from the Champions League").

`season_uefa_new_clubs` gives the new clubs of each entry round from the row's sizes: the clubs of the round, less the winners of the round below in the path, less the Champions League losers that enter it. ctest `season_uefa` checks the numbers of 2009/10 and 2018/19.

## The places (season_uefa_seeding.c)

`Uefa_Seeding::update_season` 0x9035a0 runs once a year (this+0x439 holds the year) from the constructors and new-season steps of the three cups (0x583881, 0x584e56, 0x63304d, 0x6339df, 0x902377, 0x902fac). It sorts the nations (0x903b50; entry index = rank, the rank goes to the national team club +0x1df at 0x903ca3), makes the 12-byte club records (0x904040, sorted by 0x905ce0) and calls the three place functions, each `thiscall (records, short count) ret 8` with one caller: 0x904210 (0x90375d), 0x904580 (0x90376a), 0x904e30 (0x903777). They write club +0x1db (cup record id) and +0x1df (entry code) and count each club at entry +0x11 (the coefficient divisor, 0x903c00). Their brackets are in code (0x904219..0x9042df, 0x9045b6..0x9045fc). Entry +0xc and +0xd are read only by `get_nation_places` 0x905c60 (table marks 0x6853f5, Serie A play-offs 0x641af7, board 0x6a3a32).

In a season of the access list, the wrap of 0x904210 gives all places and the other two give none (0x904e30 frees the English volunteer list at this+0x43b as 0x904fc0..0x904fe1). Order:

1. Champions League places by league position: the p-th club of the nation (picker 0x9050d0 with skip placed 0, as 0x904465).
2. The Champions League title holder: the last winner in the history (0x4afd80 cdecl (comp record, k), as 0x9042e3), in the group stage when it has no place.
3. The Europa League title holder: a Champions League place from 2015/16 (play-offs to 2017/18, groups from 2018/19), a Europa League group place before, when it has no place.
4. Europa League places: the first to the cup winner (league table entry +0x1e, as 0x9047e2; the runner-up when the winner has a place), the last to the league cup winner (+0x22, as 0x904ad8) for England and France, the others to the first club with no place (0x9050d0 with skip placed 1).
5. Fair play (2009/10 to 2015/16): three nations at random (0x90cfc0, as 0x904670) get one Q1 place more.
6. The cascade: from the group stage down, each entry round gets the new clubs that `season_uefa_new_clubs` gives. A round with too many moves its lowest-ranked club down its path. A round with too few takes the best-ranked club of the rounds below in its path, the Champions Path first for the group stage (the access lists give a free group place to the best-ranked champion). The lowest round gives up its extra clubs and fills a gap with the first club with no place of the best-ranked nations, as the UEFA Cup's fillers (0x902cb0).

Entry codes (club +0x1df): Champions League 1 groups, 2 PO LP, 3 PO CP, 4 Q3 LP, 5 Q3 CP, 6 Q2 LP, 7 Q2 CP, 8 Q1, 9 PR; Europa League 11 groups, 12 PO, 13 Q3, 14 Q2, 15 Q1, 16 PR. The Europa League codes are apart, because a Champions League loser keeps its code there (0x585a6a writes only +0x1db). The group pots sort by code, then reputation (0x586f60).

## Champions League (season_uefa_qual.c)

The original wants 71 clubs with codes 1 to 4 (build_entrants 0x584fa0: count 0x584faa, caps 0x585095..0x585135), 55 entry slots (0x584990, 0x5849a5) and 3 rounds (0x583c50 stage -1, 0x583c9b). In a season of the access list:

- **build_entrants** (wrapped): the list +0x14 holds the group clubs, then the qualifying clubs from the lowest round (PR, Q1, Q2 CP, Q2 LP, Q3 CP, Q3 LP, PO CP, PO LP). List byte +4 as the original's tiers (7, 8, 13, 0). The old list is freed as 0x584fd1 does.
- **The entry table** (wrapped): the qualifying clubs in list order, then the group clubs after the first 16. Byte +4 is the round of entry.
- **The round table** (season_uefa.c's wrap of 0x583c50 calls `season_uefa_cl_qual_table`): one round per round the row plays, from the original's Q2 record. Codes 250 Q1, 260 Q2, 270 Q3, 220 PO (the codes only name the round, 0x4b4850; slice 22's names table gives the real names). The wrap writes the round count and the name id through the caller's pointers as words and the fourth as a dword, as the original does (0x583c8a..0x583ca7): set_up_rules 0x858e70 passes +0x3c and +0x3a, so a dword name would cover the round count.
- **Dates.** A game in a moved season starts on 11 July (the first day of the rules report of a 2010/11 game). The date function moves a match to the nearest day of the round's weekday, up to 3 days either way (0x549f70, called from 0x521ed3; in a 2010/11 game 29 August gave Wednesday 1 September with weekday 2, and 30 August Friday 27 August with weekday 4). The original passes weekday 2 for its Champions League rounds (0x583cef..0x583d0e) and 3 for the UEFA Cup's (0x9026a4). The Champions League groups start on 12 September (weekday 1, 0x583f41..0x583f5e), so the qualifying must end by then. A date d with weekday w moves by the same days as d + n with weekday w + n. So the first legs of a cup are 14 days apart, all on 7-day steps: Champions League 18 July, 1, 15 and 29 August (Wednesdays), second legs 7 days later. The Europa League rounds are the day after (Thursdays), so both move by the same days, so each starts after the Champions League round whose losers it takes, and its play-offs end after the Champions League's, so the hand-over of the play-off losers comes before the Europa League group draw. The real calendar starts in late June.
- **The preliminary rounds (2018/19 on).** The Champions League one is a tournament of single matches, as the real one (rows uefa-138, uefa-139: "PR 4", one winner): semi-finals (code 240) on the Sunday and a final (code 230, a round of the winners only) on the Tuesday before the first qualifying round. `season_uefa_new_clubs` counts a quarter of its clubs as winners, so Q1 takes 31 new clubs and the drops count 3 losers. The Europa League one has two legs, on the Sunday and the Tuesday before its first qualifying round (+0x22 = 2). So the entrants match the access list (79 and 213 in 2018/19).
- **The group draw** `draw_phase1_groups` 0x585290 takes list slots 0 to 15 and the entry slots with byte +5 = 1 (0x585358..0x5853ba, exactly 32). The play-off winners get +5 = 1 at the last round (0x51af97). The group clubs after the first 16 get +5 = 1 in the wrap of map_fixture_tree. Their slots follow every slot a round uses, so the completion test (0x51b039..0x51b06d) does not read them.
- **The paths.** `map_fixture_tree` 0x518790 (vtable +0x94, called at 0x5838df and 0x584f14) makes every tie of every round at the season start. A tie (0x35 bytes, arrays at +0xad) holds two entry slots (+0, +2, -1 for a winner to come), the source of each side (+0xa/+0xe home, +0xc/+0x10 away: tie and round) and where its winner goes (+6 tie, +8 1 home or 0 away, +0x12 round), all words (0x518ef1..0x518fa7). The wrap writes the ties again after the original: Champions Path ties first (champions only: new champions and the winners of the Champions Path ties below, or of all ties below when that round has one path), then League Path ties. Each winner meets a new club when it can, as the seeded draw 0x51d2d0 does; the rest meet each other at random. Each round has flag 0x200 at +0xd, so the draw 0x51c410 keeps the ties (0x51c5d4..0x51c5f1) and sends no draw news (0x51c667). The ties of a season are known from its start, which is close to the real draws (Q1 and Q2 drawn together in June).

## Europa League (season_uefa_qual.c, season_uefa_cup.c)

- **build_entrants** (season_uefa_cup.c's wrap of 0x902b90 calls `season_uefa_uc_entrants`, in place of the original): the entry table has the qualifying clubs by round from the lowest, each round with slots after its clubs for the Champions League losers it takes, then the group clubs, then 16 slots for the original's hand-over of the Champions League play-off losers (count - 0x18, 0x585a02, in draw_phase1_groups; no count test there), then the 8 slots of the Champions League group thirds (count - 8, 0x586282). The list +0x14 holds the group clubs (byte +4 3, as 0x902deb); the hand-overs add to it. +0xb2 (the original's extra round) is 0.
- **The round table** (the wrap of 0x9025c0): one round per round of the row, from the original's qualifying round record (code 0xdc), seeded draws (0x400; 0x51d2d0 sorts by entry +4, so the new clubs of a round, with the higher tier, meet the winners) that avoid two clubs of one nation (+0xb 4). First legs 19 July, 2, 16 and 30 August (Thursdays, weekday 3). Each draw comes after the Champions League round whose losers it takes.
- **Report.** The rules report counts the clubs of both cups (`season_uefa_report_clubs`): the Champions League list (76 or 79), the clubs in the Europa League slots at the end (the entrants and the Champions League clubs that came to it). The list is not in the save (the save writes +0xa3, +0xa7 and the ties +0xad, 0x521196..0x521501), so a report after a load counts what the load rebuilds.
- **Losers into the Europa League.** A Champions League loser's entry byte +5 is -1 (0x51b5cd), and a winner's stays 0, so the loser of a tie is the side that is not in the next round's tie (+6/+8/+0x12). The wrap of `Cup::update_for_date` 0x51f890 (slot 5 of the UEFA Cup, 0x9707a4) puts the losers of each decided Champions League round into their Europa League slots (the Champions League layout comes from its round table +0xa3 and entry table +0xa7, which the save keeps, and the entry codes of the clubs; each source has fixed slots by the losers it gives, so the order does not depend on which round ends first), as the hand-over does (entry, list +0x14, +0x36, club +0x1db = the UEFA Cup id, 0x585a32..0x585a6a). A round with an empty slot waits one day at a time (0x51c46d, 0x51c9ab), so a slot filled late is safe when the counts are right. The play-off losers come with the original's hand-over. League Path Q3 losers for the groups (2018/19 on) take the slots from count - 9 down, so the hand-over from count - 0x18 never meets them.
- **The group stage** (season_uefa_cup.c): the play-off winners (+5 = 1) and every club in the slots after the rounds and before the thirds; 12 groups of 4, 6 match days (17 September to 17 December, Thursdays of 2009), group names "Group A" to "Group L" (0x41f + group: the name table at 0x4b9a44 gives "Group A" to "Group P" for 0x41f to 0x42e; 0x43d + group gives "Phase 1 Group A", the original's Champions League phase 1). The round of 32: the 12 winners and 4 Champions League thirds are seeded, the 12 runners-up and 4 other thirds are not (inference: the European seed 0x586f60 stands in for the group record of the real draw).

## Intertoto Cup

`Intertoto_Cup::build_entrants` 0x6336c0 shows a box with fewer than 60 clubs (0x6336d7, 0x6337a0), and its constructor (0x632fb0) makes a whole edition at a new game. In a season whose row has no Intertoto Cup, the seeding gives it no club, the wrap of 0x6336c0 returns 1 with no entrants, the wrap of its update_for_date 0x633d40 (slot 5, ret 8) does nothing (no draw, no match, no hand-over at 0x633deb), and the kind `uefa` skips its start_new_season (season_change_skip in a before hook).

## Super Cup (season_uefa_super_cup.c)

`EurSuperCup::create_round_table` 0x57a3c0 sets 24 August and the venue flags 0x8000 (0x57a48d) through 0x521eb0, which writes them to the round +0x13 (0x521f08). `Stadium::set_fixture_venue` 0x85c260 (from 0x51ceb6) reads 0x8000 as the home ground (club +0x69) of the club key [0x9d0264], "AS Monaco" (0x85c346..0x85c37a, set at 0x615394), and 0x10 as the stadium record of the cup +0x24 ([0xae23b0] + id * 78, 0x85c5a7..0x85c5d3; the Comp constructor sets -1 at 0x48c5df and the save keeps it at 0x5212a4). The wrap sets the row's date in every season game, and for a city other than Monaco looks for the stadium by name in [0xae23b0] (count [0xae2358], name +4): the whole name, the name before a bracket, the name in the bracket. When the database holds it, flags 0x10 and +0x24 its index; else the original's venue, with a log line.

## Decisions for Max (recommended answers taken)

1. **Ties fixed at the season start.** The Champions League qualifying ties of a season are made at its start (paths need control of the tree, see "The paths"), not at the draw dates. The Europa League rounds are drawn on their dates by the original's seeded draw.
2. **Europa League paths.** From 2018/19 the Europa League qualifying has a Champions Path of Champions League losers; here each round is one draw. The round sizes are the real ones.
3. **Preliminary rounds.** Played (lead decision, Q369 and Q293): the Champions League one as single matches on the Sunday and Tuesday, the Europa League one with two legs on the same days, all before the first qualifying round. The calendar starts on 15 July, not in late June.
4. **Path names on screen.** The round names name the round, not the path: the Champions Path and League Path ties of a round share one name.
5. **Seeding.** The access list gives the places; the draws use the game's tiers and the European seed, not the club coefficient, which the original does not keep (the seed of 0x586f60 is the entry code, then reputation).
6. **Liechtenstein.** A rank of the list with no places (Liechtenstein, which has no league) is left out; the game gives that rank the places of the next bracket. A nation with no league clubs gets no club, and the cascade fills the round.
7. **Round names.** Only the rounds that are new get a name of their season. The Champions League's Q1 to Q3 keep the original's names, as their real names did not change (Q370).

## The group draw guard (box Database 13126, 2015/16 in a full jump)

A full jump from 2001 with changing rules on stopped on day 247 of 2015 (4 September) at "Database..cpp 13126", in the Champions League group draw. All 32 clubs that enter the draw were valid clubs (a check of the list slots 0 to 15 and the entries with +5 = 1 found no bad pointer). The empty row comes from the original's placement.

`EuropeanCup::draw_phase1_groups` 0x585290 sorts the 32 clubs by entry code up and reputation down (qsort 0x945333, compare 0x586f60, 0x585435..0x58543f), pairs each club with the next unpaired club of its nation (0x58544c..0x585496), and for each pot of 8 takes the clubs in random order (0x90cfc0 at 0x585564). A club with no half gets half 2 or half 1 by the places left in each half of the pot (locals [esp+0x12] and [esp+0x13], 4 each less the clubs that already have a half, 0x585528..0x585550; random number below their sum at 0x5855b7, half 2 below the first, 0x5855bf..0x5855d1). Its partner gets the other half (0x5855e1..0x585656). The club goes in the first free slot of its pot in the four groups of its half (0x58565a..0x585699), and the pointer of the entry is zeroed only then (0x585693). A pot has 4 slots in each half. When 5 clubs of a pot have a partner in an earlier pot and those partners all sit in one half, 5 clubs need the 4 slots of one half: one club is not put anywhere, and one group row stays empty. The group draw news then reads that club's nation (0x5857f4, 0x9058b0, 0x53b3d0, box at 0x53b407).

The base game cannot meet it often (its 71 clubs have fixed nations), the access list can: a nation can have clubs in several pots (code 1 clubs in pots 1 to 3, a play-off winner in pot 4).

Fix (`season_uefa_qual.c`, `cl_group_draw_guard`): before the draw of a Champions League of the access list, the same sort (the game's qsort on a buffer on the game's stack) and the same pairs. When a pot has 5 or more clubs with a partner in an earlier pot, a search gives each club a half (partners opposite, 4 of each half in every pot), and a hook on the random function (`win_random_hook` in `user32.c`) returns the number that gives that half at the call at 0x5855b7. The other random numbers and their count stay the original's. A draw that cannot fail runs as the original's. `RECOMP_DRAW_GUARD=force` makes every draw use the halves (a test switch).

## A round drawn after its first leg date (box Cup.cpp 1278, 2018/19)

The release gate (Select All, October 2018, changing rules on) stopped on day 243 of 2018 at "Cup..cpp 1278" in the Europa League. A debug line at the call of `FIXTURE_MANAGER::find_next_fixture` 0x5ac920 from `Cup::process_fixture` 0x51a150 (return 0x51a615) gave the round table at the box: round 3 (code 270) draw day 241, first leg day 227; round 4 (code 220) draw day 246, first leg day 241; today day 245.

The original. `Cup::draw_round` 0x51c410 waits for a round with an empty slot of a tie (0x51c47c..0x51c4b1): it puts the draw date of the round (+0) a day on (0x51c4e9, date function 0x549ef0), each day, and leaves the date of the first leg (+3, +5). The Europa League slots after the first round fill from the Champions League rounds (`put_losers`), and the Champions League rounds of a game that starts on 14 July end about two weeks later than the real ones (the preliminary winner's path waits for its Q1 tie). So a round is drawn after its first leg date. `Cup::create_tie_fixtures` 0x51cb90 makes no fixture for a leg that is in the past, so the second leg has no fixture, the first leg is played, and `Cup::process_fixture` finds no second leg (0x51a610) and shows the box at 0x51a672 (line 0x4fe).

Fix (`cup_dates_ahead` in `season_uefa_qual.c`, before each day of a Europa League or Conference League of the access list, in the wrap of `Cup::update_for_date` 0x51f890): a round with no drawn tie (all tie states 0) whose draw is due (the next day at the latest) and whose first leg is less than 4 days away gets its first leg a week from today, on its match day (`cup_set_round_match_date` 0x521eb0, weekday 3); the second leg follows by the gap of the round. A round on time is not touched. The log line "is drawn late" shows each move.

The game of the gate reaches the box only on the exact build of main 17f5292 (later commits move memory and the game takes another path), so the fix was run on 17f5292 with a debug wrap (the box came after 1,202 s) and again with the fix: no box, the 12 groups drawn and the Champions League round of 16 drawn (day 327 of 2018). The same run on the fixed build of main 5b3153a passes 31 August (day 306).
