## The era

Rows (Q520, as slice 20): from 2014/15 the rows "Serie C group A", "B" and "C" take the comp_file of the object they play in (`ita_ser_c1a`, `ita_ser_c1b`, `ita_ser_c2a`), and the C1 and C2 rows of those seasons are `n/a` with a note. So slice 18's sizes and places, slice 15's names table (the groups are named "Lega Pro girone A" to 2016/17 and "Serie C girone A" from 2017/18) and the rules report read them as the rows of these objects, with no mapping in code.

A season S is in the era when its rules season has the three rows "Serie C group A", "B" and "C" (held, 2 to 20 clubs). The rules season is S with changing rules on and the start year with changing rules off (Q232). The base game and `RECOMP_SEASON_CODE=off` have no rules season (`season_game_changes`), so they never enter the era (Q152, Q342). After the last row of the files (2027/28) the last row holds (Q248, as slice 18's `row_of_season`), so the era never ends: the original's chain, exchange and regroup read C2/B and C2/C whenever C2/A exists (0x642506..0x642602, 0x6421dc, 0x6427aa), and a game without the flag has no C2/B. For the same reason the era's code also runs whenever the C2/A object exists without C2/B.

## The objects (Q620)

Groups A, B and C are the objects of Serie C1/A (ItaSerC1a, key id [0x9cf578]), C1/B ([0x9cf57c]) and C2/A ([0x9cf580]). Each class picks its clubs by the key id of its own record (0x64b71d, 0x64f8cd, 0x653b3d), so each group plays the clubs whose division is its record.

- `create_italian_comps` 0x835320 always makes C1/A and C1/B, and C2/A, C2/B and C2/C only with the extra division flag (bit 4 of nation +0x11c, 0x835407). Q363: the single Serie C follows C1, so it always exists.
- Without the flag, slice 26's module makes the C2/A object (def `season_ita_group_c`, entry 21, record "Italian Serie C2/A", operator new 0xee and constructor 0x652e90 thiscall (year, record), as 0x835696..0x8356ce). Entry 21 is Italy's: `key_nation_build_table` 0x667150 stores the Italian nation at 0xb64348 = 0xb63d60 + 21 x 0x48. With the flag the original's object exists, so the make function returns 0 (at a new game and at a load). The def's file is the class's own file (`ita_ser_c2a`), so `season_change_file` and the rule kinds still read it as C2/A.
- C2/B and C2/C (with the flag) in the era: no league is made for an ended C2 division (O259, row A23). The club moves give C2/B and C2/C no clubs (`season_ita_division_size`, Q290), and at a load after the change into the era the chain and the regroup have moved their clubs out. A league of 0 clubs cannot be made (`League::create_fixtures` 0x6835c0 stops at league.cpp 1020, 0x683615..0x683638). So in a season game the operator new of C2/B and C2/C in `create_italian_comps` 0x835320 (0x835696, 0x8356e9) gives 0 when the division holds no clubs (`rt_ita_c2_none`, `new_divisions.py`); the original stores 0 in the entry slot (0x8356d5, 0x835728) and the wrap of 0x835320 drops the empty slots from the entry's array (+0x10, count +0xc). The readers of the C2/B and C2/C ids cope with no object: the set_up_rules of C2/A, C2/B and C2/C (0x653b90, 0x658050, 0x65c1e0) end their link below at a division with no clubs (`c2_link`), the awards of 0x768940 (no object gives a box at 0x76895a), 0x768cf0 and 0x7698f0 (no object gives the box nation_awards.cpp 1624 at 0x769acc, found by the 2014-c2 run) skip it, and the tier block's last call takes C2/B (`rt_ita_tier_last`). Before this the original made a placeholder league of one club that ended at once (O259 called it a box-only fix). A C2 league that ends while the game runs (the change into the era) keeps its object until the next load, with year 2999, the shared rule for a competition that never returns (Q395), and the chain and the starts never touch it (below). Its table keeps the last season, and the rules report shows that edition with year 2999.
- An ended league also leaves the league links (+0x1c the league above, +0x20 the league below). The season change pass starts a nation's leagues only through the top league (vtable +0xc, 0x48ce10, false while +0x1c is not -1). It starts them only when the top table and each table below it by +0x20 is complete (vtable +0x10, `League::check_complete` 0x689ad0, at 0x48ce30..0x48ce54). An ended league is never complete again, and with the extra division Serie A never started 2015/16. So the league above takes the ended league's link below. The ended league keeps its +0x1c, so the pass never takes it for a top league. `ItaSerC2a::set_up_rules` writes C2/A's links again each season and at a load (+0x1c [0x9cf57c], +0x20 [0x9cf584], 0x653bf5..0x653c0c), so the unlink runs again after each start of C2/A and in the rule kind's load hook.
- Names: from the names table (Q520). A small rule kind writes the news of the change into the era.

## The season change

`ItaSerA::start_new_season` 0x641700 already has slice 16's generated wrap (`season_starts.c`), so the era replaces the Italian functions it calls, each only in the era of the season that starts (ItaSerA's year + 1: it adds 1 at 0x641789, after the chain):

1. The chain (vtable +0xb0 = 0x642410, thiscall (1) ret 4):
   - the final places of each table (vtable +0xa4, `League::record_final_positions` 0x68aa30, of A, B and the groups, as 0x642423..0x642465);
   - A and B, 4 and 4 in pairs (as 0x64248e);
   - the clubs that go up to Serie B (Q627). Each group's class keeps its own places and play-off (`set_up_rules` 0x653bdd: +0xbe 1, +0xbf 4; the play-off of 0x653e90 reads +0xbf at 0x653f8f), so each group ends with a winner (status 0) and a play-off winner (status 5), 6 in all. Serie B sends down 4 (its rows of status 3). The rows that go up are, to the number of Serie B's relegated rows: the three winners, then the play-off winners by points (row + [league + 0xad] x 14 + 0x19, as `league_compare_table_rows` 0x683264..0x683275), then the next rows by points. The other rows of status 0 or 5 get 0xff, the status of a row that does not move (0x689df4). The log line "serie c: n up to Serie B (groups A a, B b, C c), m down from Serie B" shows the counts;
   - B and each group in pairs (as 0x6424b6 and 0x6424de). `league_promote_and_relegate` 0x689c80 moves the rows of status 3 of the upper league and of status 0 or 5 of the lower one, and in pairs a row moves only with a partner (0x689d26..0x689d5d). With 4 a side every relegated Serie B club goes down;
   - the relegated rows (status 3) of each group go to the Serie D data division [0x9cf58c] (`0x6831a0` thiscall (club, record, 1) ret 0xc, as `exchange_serie_d_clubs` 0x642317..0x6423e6 does for C2). Q620: the original's own lowest tier pool, so the pool never runs dry. The regroup does not take these clubs back up: the original takes its Serie D clubs up before it sends C2 clubs down (0x6422de..0x64230c, then 0x642367), and the chain keeps a list of the clubs it sends down;
   - at the change into the era (the season before was not in it): the clubs of C2/B and C2/C that their table does not relegate join group C's record, the relegated go to the Serie D, and both objects end.
2. `exchange_serie_d_clubs` 0x6421c0, `exchange_serie_c2_clubs_unplayed` 0x641fc0 and `regroup_serie_c1_clubs` 0x642630 do nothing in the era (0x641fc0 runs the regroup when there is no C2/A object).
3. The regroup (in place of `regroup_serie_c2_clubs` 0x642790, which reads 54 clubs and splits by 18):
   - the clubs of the three group records;
   - too few for the rows: clubs of the Serie D data division less the clubs just relegated, shuffled with 0x4afbd0 (as 0x6422de), then Italian clubs with no division by reputation (Q622). The pool with no division stops before the national teams, the last 2 x [0xae2350] rows of the club table (0x53b900), as the German Cup does (0x5d9569..0x5d957c);
   - too many: the lowest reputations go to the Serie D;
   - sorted by latitude (`database_compare_clubs_by_latitude` 0x53fd40 with the game's qsort, as 0x642826..0x642833); the first go to group C, then B, then A (C2/C was the first of 0x64284a, the southern group);
   - a human manager whose club changes group gets the news of `regroup_serie_c1_clubs` (0x6426e5..0x642745: 0x7875e0, 0x77bbe0 (0x13, 0), 0x785790 twice, 0x7861e0).
4. Two patched loads of the C2/A object in 0x641700 (`new_divisions.py`):
   - 0x6418d5, the tier block (`League::limit_club_division_tier` 0x68a980 with (1, 3, -3, 1) on C2/A, C2/B, C2/C, else on C1/A, C1/B): in the era `rt_ita_tier_c2` calls it on C2/A and gives 0, so the block does C1/A and C1/B (0x641915). All three groups get it.
   - 0x641986, the start block (vtable +8 of C2/A, C2/B, C2/C, 0x64198d..0x6419b7): in the era `rt_ita_start_c2` starts C2/A and gives 0, so C2/B and C2/C are never started (0x6419ba).

   Outside the era both give the object, so the original runs.

## The cups in the era

## The two-group Serie C2, 2011/12 to 2013/14 (Q529)

With the extra division flag the original plays C2 in three groups of 18. The rows of 2011/12 to 2013/14 hold two groups: 20 and 21 clubs in 2011/12 (ita-176, ita-177), 18 and 18 in 2012/13 and 2013/14 (ita-193, ita-194, ita-210, ita-211), and C2/C not held (ita-178, ita-195, ita-212). A season is a two-group season when its rules season (Q232) has the C2/A and C2/B rows and no C2/C row, the flag is set and it is not in the Serie C era. Without the flag C2 has no objects and the original's code runs (Q363: no C2 is made). The base game and `RECOMP_SEASON_CODE=off` have no rules season, so they never get any of it (Q152, Q342).

- **C2/C ends** as the era's ended groups do (year 2999, Q623; `idle`): it keeps its last table and leaves the league links. ItaSerC2b::set_up_rules writes C2/B's link below to C2/C each season (+0x20 = [0x9cf588], 0x6580bf..0x6580cc), so the link ends there (`c2_link` after each set-up, `unlink_ended` at a load). At a new game or a load of a two-group season no league is made for C2/C, whose division holds no clubs (row A23 above).
- **Sizes.** C2/A and C2/B hold the clubs of their rows, at most 20: the 21 of C2/B in 2011/12 is 20, as the era's groups of 20 (one table block of 20 rows). The table blocks of C2/A (0x653a9b) and C2/B (0x657f5b) take 20 rows, the count stores (0x653aa2, 0x657f62) take the clubs of the division, and a group of more than 18 gets the era's 38 match days (`create_schedule` of C2/A 0x6531a0 and C2/B 0x657660, D 0x22 at 0x65769d). A count store that changes the count sets the matches of a club (+0xa7) back to -1, also when the new count is the original's 18: the groups of 20 of 2011/12 kept 38 into 2012/13, `League::check_complete` 0x689ad0 never found them complete, and Serie A never started 2013/14 (the first test game).
- **Places.** The classes keep 1 direct place and a play-off of 4 whose winner goes up (+0xbe 1, +0xbf 4: C2/A 0x653bdd, 0x653be3; C2/B 0x65809d, 0x6580a3). The status byte of a row (`League::update_positions_and_status` 0x684cd0) is 5 for the champion, 0 promoted, 1 a promotion play-off place, 3 relegated, 0xfe nothing to play for, and `league_promote_and_relegate` 0x689c80 moves up only the rows of status 0 and 5 (0x689d47..0x689d4e). The class's play-off takes places 2 to 5 whatever the direct places are: a test with 3 direct places gave the statuses 5, 0xfe, 0, 0xfe, 0xfe, 1, 1 (the champion, the play-off's winner and losers, two play-off places that never played). So each group sends 2 up. With three groups that is the 6 that C1 sends down (1 direct and 2 play-out losers a group); with two it is 4, the pairs of 0x689c80 left 2 relegated clubs in C1 (the first test game: "6 down from Serie C1, 4 up from Serie C2"), and C1's regroup takes a fixed 36 (0x64269c). The rows give each group 2 direct places and 1 by the play-off. So the chain gives status 0 to the best row by place of each group (A, then B) that does not go up and is not relegated, until C2 sends up as many as C1 sends down (the log line's "by place"). 2013/14 needs none: at the change into the era no C2 club goes up. The era's chain gives the rows of status 0 and 5 of C2/A (group C, a C2 group the season before) the status 0xff, so they do not go up to Serie B (the C2 rows of 2013/14 send no club up, ita-210, ita-211).
- **The season change** (ItaSerA::start_new_season 0x641700):
  1. The chain 0x642410: at the change into two groups (C2/C still plays its last season) the original runs, with its three C2 groups. From then on (C2/C has ended) the runtime makes the original's calls less the two of C2/C: the final places of A, B, C1/A, C1/B, C2/A and C2/B (0x642423..0x642465, 0x6424fb, 0x642511), then league_promote_and_relegate 0x689c80 with the original's arguments: A and B (1, 1, 4, 4; 0x64248e), B and C1/A, B and C1/B (1, 1, -1, -1; 0x6424b6, 0x6424de), C1/A and C2/A (1, 1; 0x642550), C1/B and C2/A (0, 1; 0x642578), C1/A and C2/B (1, 1; 0x6425a0), C1/B and C2/B (0, 1; 0x6425c8). The log line "serie c2: n down from Serie C1 (A a, B b), m up from Serie C2 (A x, B y)" gives the counts before the C1 and C2 pairs.
  2. The Serie D exchange 0x6421c0: the relegated rows (status 3) of each C2 group that still plays go to the Serie D (as 0x642317..0x6423e6, each group to its own count; the original loops to C2/A's count for all three, 0x64231d). The 9 clubs of 0x6422da do not come up here: the regroup fills each group to its row from the Serie D, less the clubs just relegated (as the era's regroup, Q622).
  3. The regroup 0x642790 (54 clubs in groups of 18, the first by latitude to C2/C, 0x64284a..0x642864): the clubs of C2/A, C2/B and C2/C to the counts of the rows, filled from the Serie D (shuffled with 0x4afbd0), then from the Italian clubs with no division by reputation, or cut by reputation to the Serie D; sorted by latitude (0x53fd40), the first to C2/B (the southern group), the rest to C2/A. A human manager whose club changes group gets the news of 0x6426e5..0x642745. At the change into two groups C2/C then ends. The log line is "serie c2 2011/12: 40 clubs in groups of 20 and 20 (...)".
  4. C1/A and C1/B get no call, as in the original with the flag.
