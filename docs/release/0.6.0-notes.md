# CM 01/02 Bosman Edition 0.6.0

<!-- TODO slice 30: the publish date. These notes are the body of the GitHub release. -->

Play any season from 2001/02 to 2025/26. Each season starts with its own squads and plays with its own leagues, cups and rules. In a long game the rules change each July, as they did in real life.

Downloads for Mac, Windows and Linux (the Linux AppImage also runs on the Steam Deck in desktop mode). Your saved games and `settings.txt` stay where they are.

## Start a game in any season

1. Start the game. At the top of Setup Game, a strip shows the season, its database and whether the rules change.
2. Click the strip. The Choose Season screen opens with a tile for each season, in rows for the 2000s, 2010s and 2020s.
3. Click a tile. Under the tiles you see the database and who made it, the European format of that season and the start date.
4. Set Changing rules on or off for this game, under the season details.
5. Click Start. The first time you pick a season, the game unpacks its database into your save folder. This takes a short time once.

The game starts in July of that season. Every player has the right age for that July, and the competition history lists the real winners up to that season.

## Databases

Every season has one database, and all of them are in the download:

- 2001/02 and 2002/03: the official 3.9.68 data.
- 2004/05 and 2006/07: our own Summer 2004 and Summer 2006, with facts from public sources and ratings by the CM 01/02 Update Team.
- Every other season: a database of the CM 01/02 Update Team, the earliest one of that season that players did not report as faulty.

Thanks to the Update Team for their databases. More versions of each season are on their forum at champman0102.co.uk, and the Choose Season screen has a button that opens it.

To play a database folder of your own, put it in `Databases` in your save folder and use the Custom database tile. You say which season it holds, and that season's rules apply.

## Each season plays by its own rules

- Leagues: the number of clubs, the places up and down, play-offs and splits of that season, for all divisions of the 27 nations in the game. The 3. Liga, the Primera Federación, Liga 3, the single Serie C in Italy and Brazil's state championships are in. If a season's database lacks a real top or second tier club, the game adds it with a squad.
- Domestic cups: replays or none, one leg or two, group stages and four-club super cups, as in that season.
- Substitutes, bench size, foreign player rules, work permits and transfer windows of that season. From 1 January 2021, players from the EU need a work permit in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.
- Names: competitions and rounds carry the names they had that season, such as the Championship or the Europa League.
- European cups:
  - 2001/02 and 2002/03: two Champions League group stages, a knockout UEFA Cup and the Intertoto Cup in summer.
  - From 2003/04: one Champions League group stage, then a round of 16.
  - From 2004/05: the UEFA Cup has eight groups of five.
  - From 2009/10: the Europa League replaces the UEFA Cup, and the Intertoto Cup ends.
  - From 2021/22: the Conference League starts, and away goals no longer decide a tie.
  - From 2024/25: all three cups start with a league phase of 36 clubs.
  - The places of each nation come from that season's UEFA access list.
- International football: the Nations League from 2018/19, Euros of 24 nations from 2016, the 2022 World Cup in November and December, and the 48-team World Cup of 2026 with its qualifying. Also the CONCACAF Nations League, the Gold Cup and the Finalissima.

The full list of rule changes, by nation and season, is in [0.6.0-rule-changes.md](https://github.com/maxevans60/cm0102-bosman-edition/blob/v0.6.0/docs/release/0.6.0-rule-changes.md).

## A long game moves with the years

With Changing rules on, each rule changes in the July it changed in real life. A news item at the start of the season lists what changed. This also works in a game that starts in 2001/02: you see the First Division become the Championship in 2004, Serie A grow to 20 clubs and five substitutes come in.

After 2025/26 the changes that are already announced with a date come in, for example the Championship play-offs for 3rd to 8th from 2026/27. Then the rules stay as they are.

With Changing rules off, the rules of your start season stay for the whole game. The save keeps the setting, and you cannot change it after the start. The Game tab of Bosman Edition Options sets the default for a new game.

Saves from 0.5.0 and earlier load with Changing rules off.

## Also new

- Team views: Best XI and Squad depth are one screen, in the look of the original game. The pitch lies on its side, with a card for each of the 11 places that shows the Best XI player and the next two for that place. Half stars show how well each player fits. A player who cannot play the next match has a tag: Inj, Int (national team) or Out.
- Compare players has the look of the original comparison screen. The higher number of each pair shows in that player's colour, and the polygon stays.
- Setup Game has a new layout. Hall Of Fame, Game Credits and Web Sites are on a new Extras screen. The start screens have the title "Championship Manager: Bosman Edition".
- Bosman Edition Options: the Screen tab is called Graphics. The Save folder row has a Change button that opens your computer's folder picker. The new folder applies from the next start, and saved games already in the old folder stay there.
- Display report: if the picture looks wrong, press Ctrl+Shift+D (Cmd+Shift+D on a Mac). The game copies a report of your screens and window to the clipboard and saves it as `display-report.txt` in the save folder. Paste it into a bug report. It does not contain your account name.
- Windows: text stays sharp with display scaling at 125 % or 150 %.
- HD mode is gone. Widescreen fills a large screen with sharp, bigger pixels. A `display = hd` line in `settings.txt` now gives widescreen.
- In a language other than English, the game no longer stops a new game or a load with the error box "Database..cpp 7784". New games on the 3.9.60 and 3.9.65 data show names in your language. Season databases keep the English names. The "CD Not Required" box no longer shows in other languages either.
- Seasons are written in full, for example "2009/10" in place of "2009/0".
- The 0.5.1 Mac fix is in every download: a game with Brazil selected can save its state championships.
- The Linux download is called `CM0102-Bosman-Edition-0.6.0-x86_64.AppImage`.

## Known limits

- The bench holds at most 9 players, because the match engine holds 9. A real bench of 12 plays as 9.
- The MLS stays at 12 clubs, so the MLS expansion clubs do not play.
- Careers that a season database never updated stop in 2001.
- In databases from September 2009 on, most players joined their club in 2008.
- The award history keeps the years that the database gives it.

## Report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub, or post in the Bosman Edition thread on the champman0102 forum. Say which computer you use and what you clicked before the problem. For a picture or window problem, press Ctrl+Shift+D (Cmd+Shift+D on a Mac) and paste the display report. Fixes go into 0.6.1.
